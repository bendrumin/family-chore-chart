Version 2.0 gives the cents somewhere to go, and gives kids a screen that is really theirs.

• Goals: kids pick what they are saving for and watch the bar fill as their unspent allowance grows. You get a nudge when it is full, then pay it out from their page
• Reward Store: price the things money cannot buy (screen time, pick dinner, stay up late). Kids ask with a tap, you say yes or no from Needs Your OK, and the price comes off their balance
• Chores on their own days: pick the days a chore is due. Today's list, the week grid, and the widget only show what is due, and a day with nothing due never breaks a streak
• Kids see their streak, this week's earnings, and their badges on their dashboard
• Kid mode wears your theme: header, celebration, confetti, widgets, and Live Activity follow your seasonal or accent theme
• Optional approval: turn on Approve Chores First and kids' ticks wait for your OK (with a tap-to-approve alert). Any chore can also ask for a photo
• The medium widget shows each kid's goal progress; the small one shows the family's best streak
