Version 2.5.3: money jars, team challenges, and routines for younger kids.

• Spend, Save, Give (Premium): split what each kid earns into three jars, pay a weekly allowance on your day, add interest on Save, take off a fine with a reason, set up a weekly payback, and reward screen-time minutes. Kids see their jars and money news on their own screen
• Family challenges (Premium): siblings team up for a shared reward, like 50 chores this week for pizza night, with a team progress bar and confetti when they get there
• Read-aloud routines, free for everyone: a speaker button reads each step out loud, and a routine can read every step by itself
• Picture steps, a calm finish-by timer, Build with AI, and a Happening now view of who is mid-routine (Premium)
• Family calendar feed (Premium): add chores and routines to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook
• Appearance has its own screen: light or dark, your theme and accent colour, all in Settings > Appearance instead of filling the whole Settings page
• Finished routines with a reward now add that money to your kid's balance
