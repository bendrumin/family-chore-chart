Version 2.4 adds Sign in with Apple.

• Create your family in one tap with Sign in with Apple, with no password to remember
• Choose Hide My Email and ChoreStar never sees your real address
• New families are asked what to call themselves first, so kids see your family name when they log in
• Already signed up with email? Nothing changes: keep signing in with your email and password
• Deleting your account also removes ChoreStar from your Apple ID's sign-in list
• Made for iPhone Duo: open it for a two-column kid mode and a routine player that shows every step beside the current one. Half-open, nothing sits on the fold
• Small fixes and polish
