Version 1.6 fixes chore deletion.

• Fixed: deleting a chore could remove the wrong chore from the list, leave a "deleted" chore that came back, or crash the app — deletes now always confirm the chore you picked and remove exactly that one
• Fixed: if a delete fails (for example, while offline), the app now tells you instead of failing silently
