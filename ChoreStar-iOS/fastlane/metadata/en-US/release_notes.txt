Version 2.6: Star Pets.

• Star Pets: each kid adopts a pet on their own screen, names it, and watches it grow with every chore and routine. Every family gets a pup. Premium adds a kitty, bunny, dino, panda and owl, hats and extras to unlock as they level up, a new prize every month, and growing all the way up
• See every kid's pet on Home, and turn pets on or off for each kid in Settings > Star Pets
• Once you're set up, Home points out one feature your family hasn't tried yet, one at a time
