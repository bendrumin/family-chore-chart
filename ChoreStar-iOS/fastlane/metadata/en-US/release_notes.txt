Version 1.5 adds a Week view to the Chores tab and improves accessibility throughout the app.

• Week at a glance: a new Week segment on the Chores tab shows each child's full week of chores, with a quick switcher between children
• Accessibility: better VoiceOver support, larger touch targets, Dynamic Type on titles, and higher-contrast text in both light and dark mode
• Fixed: weekly summary stats no longer wrap onto two lines
