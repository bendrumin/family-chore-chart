Version 1.7 helps you feel progress without opening the app.

• Activity alerts: tap a push when a kid finishes all chores or a routine to jump straight to their page. Turn these on or off in Settings
• Widget deep links: tap Today's Progress (or a child on the medium widget) to open the right place in ChoreStar
• New Lock Screen rectangular widget shows who's ahead on today's chores
