Version 1.4 adds photo avatars, push notifications, and chores for children to check off themselves.

• Photo avatars: take or choose a photo for a child, then decorate it with any of 42 stickers
• Push notifications: you are notified when a child finishes all of their chores for the day, or completes a routine
• Children can now check off their own chores after signing in with their PIN
• A custom accent color now syncs between iPhone and chorestar.app
• Fixed: families using a flat daily rate now see the correct amount for the day
• Fixed: notifications now appear while the app is open

Questions or feedback are welcome at hi@chorestar.app
