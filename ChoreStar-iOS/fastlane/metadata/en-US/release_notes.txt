Version 1.9 is about the kid's screen: it now shows what matters to them, and it wears your family's colors.

• Chores on their own days: pick the days a chore is due (Every day, Weekdays, Weekends, or any mix). Today's list, the week grid, and the widget only show what is due
• Perfect days that are fair: a day with nothing due never breaks a streak or costs the week bonus
• Kids see their streak, this week's earnings, and their badges right on their dashboard, with a badge cabinet showing what to earn next
• Kid mode wears your theme: the header, celebration, confetti, widgets, and Live Activity follow the seasonal or accent theme you picked. Halloween arrives in October on its own
• Optional approval: turn on Approve Chores First in Settings and kids' ticks wait in a Needs Your OK list (with a tap-to-approve alert on your phone) before they count
• Ask for a photo on any chore: kids snap it from their list, you see it in the tray
• The small widget shows the family's best streak
