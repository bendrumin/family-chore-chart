Version 2.6.3: kids can ask for a Star Pet.

• When a kid taps a Star Pet they don't have yet, they can ask you for it. You get a notification and see "Maya wants the owl" in Needs Your OK, with Not now if it isn't the right time
• Premium families with more than one kid get a tip about chores that take turns: a rotating chore moves to the next kid every week, and a bonus chore goes to whoever finishes first
• Bigger tap targets on Not now buttons
