Version 1.8 makes it easy to spread the word, and easier to get started.

• Rate ChoreStar: Settings > About now has a one-tap link to leave an App Store rating or review
• Share ChoreStar: send the App Store link to another family straight from Settings
• New families choose how rewards work during setup (one daily amount, or an amount per chore) instead of starting from a preset
• Copy polish throughout the app
