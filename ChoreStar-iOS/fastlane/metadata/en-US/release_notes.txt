Version 2.3 puts chores on your Home Screen and the chart on paper.

• Tap a chore on the ChoreStar widget to mark it done, or to approve a kid's tick, without opening the app
• A new Large widget shows every kid's list for today
• Export & Print in Settings: a chore chart for the fridge and weekly templates in three styles, free for every family
• Premium adds the family report PDF and a spreadsheet of the week
• Every currency and every language prints correctly, riyals and Arabic names included
• Home Screen shortcuts: press and hold the icon to jump to Today, add a chore, or open Kid Mode
• Ticks land in the same week as on the web when your iPhone starts the week on Monday or Saturday
• Small fixes and polish
